title: Corrections
description: "When we get something wrong, we fix it quickly and say so openly."
---

- **Report an error:** [corrections email]. We reply within two working days.
- **Factual errors** are corrected in the article, with a dated note at the end: *"Correction, 12 October 2026: An earlier version said X. It is Y."*
- **Serious errors** (a wrong claim at the heart of the story) get the note at the top of the article as well.
- **Small fixes** to spelling, grammar or broken links are made without a note.
- **Updates** with new information are marked "Updated" with the date and a line on what changed.
- **Removals** are rare. If we take a piece down, its address shows a short explanation instead of disappearing.
