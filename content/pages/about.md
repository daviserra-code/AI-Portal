AI-Portal explains what is changing in artificial intelligence, why it matters and what you can do about it. We write for curious people, not insiders: professionals, students, parents and small business owners who want clear answers without the hype.

We publish a handful of carefully edited pieces a day rather than everything that happens. Our explainers, guides and glossary are kept up to date, so they stay useful long after the news moves on.
