---
term: "Artificial general intelligence (AGI)"
definition: "AI that could do most intellectual tasks a person can do, at a similar level. There is no single agreed definition, and no consensus that it exists yet."
related: [artificial-intelligence, superintelligence]
status: draft
---

Today's AI systems can be very capable in some areas and surprisingly weak in others. AGI describes a system that would be broadly capable across almost everything a person can think through: learning a new job, reasoning about an unfamiliar problem, planning over weeks.

Companies and researchers draw the line in different places, which is why claims that AGI is "close" or "here" often mean different things. When you see the term, look for how the speaker defines it.
