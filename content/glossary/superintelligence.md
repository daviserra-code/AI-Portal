---
term: "Superintelligence"
definition: "In research, a hypothetical future AI that far exceeds the best human minds in almost every field."
related: [agi, super-intelligence-si]
status: draft
---

The philosopher Nick Bostrom made the term widely known in his 2014 book *Superintelligence*. It describes something beyond [AGI](/glossary/agi): not a system as capable as a person, but one far more capable than any person.

No existing system is superintelligent in this sense. Since 29 September 2026 the US government also uses "super intelligence" as its official name for all AI, which is a different meaning. See [SI](/glossary/super-intelligence-si).
