Most AI you meet today learns patterns from large amounts of data rather than following rules a programmer wrote by hand. A chatbot that answers questions, a phone that sorts your photos by face and a bank system that flags unusual payments are all examples.

AI is good at the specific jobs it was built or trained for. It can still make confident mistakes, so it pays to check anything important it tells you.
