---
term: "AI Act (EU)"
definition: "The European Union's law on artificial intelligence. It sets rules for AI systems based on how much risk they pose to people's safety and rights."
related: [artificial-intelligence, deepfake, bias]
status: draft
---

The AI Act is a European Union regulation, so it applies across all EU countries, including Slovenia. Its main idea is that stricter rules apply where the stakes are higher. A small number of uses are banned outright. AI used in sensitive areas, such as hiring, education or access to public services, faces strict requirements. Lower-risk tools mostly have to be open about being AI. [EDITOR: check the current list of banned practices and high-risk areas against the official text before publishing]

For ordinary people, one visible effect is transparency: you should be told when you are talking to a chatbot, and AI-generated content such as [deepfakes](/glossary/deepfake) should be labelled.

The Act was adopted in 2024 and its rules take effect in stages over several years. [EDITOR: check which obligations apply as of publication date, and whether any deadlines have been changed] It does not ban AI in general, and it does not cover every use, for example most military uses.
