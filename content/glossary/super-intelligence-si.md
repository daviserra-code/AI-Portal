definition: "Since 29 September 2026, the official name US federal agencies use for what was called artificial intelligence. Legally it covers the same systems."

An executive order told agencies to replace "artificial intelligence" and "AI" with "super intelligence" and "SI" in their communications. Existing laws and contracts were not rewritten, and the term points to the existing legal definition of AI.

Outside US government documents, most people still say "AI". Read our explainer: [SI, AI or AGI? What the new US term actually means](/super-intelligence/si-ai-or-agi).
